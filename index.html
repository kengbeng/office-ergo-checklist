Assessment instructions
  1. Assess the workstation in its usual working arrangement. Answer all 28 checks and refer to the guidance under each item. Use N/A only for the four conditional questions where the equipment or activity is not used.
  2. For each No response and any reported discomfort, record the observation and proposed follow-up. Add an action owner and target date in the comments where appropriate.
  3. Identify concerns: A No response to a setup or work-practice question means an adjustment or follow-up is needed. For the discomfort question, a Yes response means prompt review is needed.
  4. Arrange follow-up: Review concerns with the Safety and Health Coordinator (SHC) and/or Business Partner (SHBP). If further review or specialist advice is needed, the SHC and/or SHBP can consult ORMC for guidance.

The concern count is a follow-up aid, not a validated risk score. An incomplete assessment cannot receive a completed outcome. No numerical referral bands are used.

Printing and saving help
  1. Select Print / Save PDF. Choose your printer or Save as PDF, and use A4 portrait.
  2. Check the preview. If browser headers or footers appear, turn off Headers and footers in the print settings.
  3. Use Download editable draft to keep a copy you can reopen and update. A PDF is the assessment record; the draft keeps the form editable.

The report includes every question, response, recommended action for each concern, comments, the outcome and signature lines. Incomplete reports are clearly marked.
